Royal Palm

Roystonea regia

About the Species

Roystonea regia, commonly known as the Cuban royal palm or Florida royal palm, is a large and majestic species of palm. Native to Mexico, parts of Central America, the Caribbean, and southern Florida, it is widely planted as a decorative tree in tropical and subtropical regions worldwide, including school gardens across India.

Wikipedia Quick Facts

  • Appearance: Mature trees have a smooth, light-grey trunk that resembles a stone column. When the tree is young, this trunk is hidden by a bright green "crownshaft" made of tightly wrapped leaf bases.
  • Leaves: It features long, pinnate (feathery) leaves that can reach up to 4 meters (13 feet) in length.
  • Height: In ideal conditions, these fast-growing palms can reach heights of 20 to 30 meters (66–98 feet).
  • Ecology: The tree's flowers provide a rich source of nectar for bees and bats, while its seeds are a food source for local bird populations.

Growth in Warm Climates

The Royal Palm thrives in full sunlight and warm temperatures, making it incredibly well-suited for the semi-arid, sunny climate of Rajasthan. They require moderate watering when young but become highly drought-tolerant as they establish their roots.
