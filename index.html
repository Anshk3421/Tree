The Bottle Palm

Hyophorbe lagenicaulis

A Botanical Marvel

The Bottle Palm is a fascinating, slow-growing palm species renowned worldwide for its unique, sculptural appearance. When mature, its trunk swells remarkably near the base and tapers upwards, creating a shape identical to an antique glass bottle. However, when the tree is young, this swelling is hidden by the tightly wrapped leaf bases.

Native Realm

Endemic solely to Round Island, a small volcanic island off the coast of Mauritius in the Indian Ocean.

Canopy

Unlike other palms that hold dozens of leaves, the Bottle Palm elegantly supports only 4 to 6 arching, feather-like fronds at a time.

Growth

A true test of patience. It is incredibly slow-growing, taking many years to form its signature swollen trunk.

Conservation: A Race Against Time

Despite being a popular ornamental plant in tropical gardens and schools around the globe, the Bottle Palm faces a tragic reality in the wild. According to the IUCN Red List, it is classified as Critically Endangered.

At one point, habitat destruction and the introduction of invasive species like rabbits and goats to Round Island decimated the wild population. Thanks to intense conservation efforts, the island is recovering, but wild Bottle Palms remain incredibly rare, making every planted specimen in gardens a living testament to its survival.
